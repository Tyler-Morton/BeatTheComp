Risk Architecture

Built to lose less

The strategy is designed around capital preservation first. Three independent guardrails sit between the optimizer and the broker.

Vol-Targeting Overlay

Continuously scales the book toward a constant 20% annual volatility, de-risking into turbulence and parking the excess in cash.

Drawdown Circuit Breaker

Halts trading if the account breaches a hard drawdown limit, with a guard against false triggers from broker timeouts.

Concentration Limit

Caps any single position's weight so one name cannot dominate the book, while exempting the cash sleeve.

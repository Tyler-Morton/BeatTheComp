Strategy

How the bot works

Four layers stacked on top of each other. Each one does a single job cleanly, and nothing ships without passing an out-of-sample backtest.

LAYER 01
Regime-picked asset universe

A growth engine of leveraged tech ETFs (TQQQ, UPRO, SOXL, TECL) when the market is healthy, rotating toward gold, treasuries, and cash when it is not. A regime detector decides which team is on the field.

TQQQ · UPRO · SOXL · GLD · TLT
LAYER 02
HRP weighting

Hierarchical Risk Parity sizes positions by correlation structure instead of trusting noisy return forecasts.

LAYER 03
Vol-targeting overlay

Scales exposure toward a 20% annual-vol target and parks the rest in cash (SHV).

Live
LAYER 04
ML crash model

A gradient-boosted classifier estimates drawdown probability daily. Running observe-only until its live track record earns it the right to size trades.

Shadow mode
INFRASTRUCTURE
Fully autonomous

Runs every morning at 6:30 AM via GitHub Actions. It pulls data, re-optimizes, applies the risk overlays, and rebalances on Alpaca with zero manual intervention. Bad broker data can't trade: a $0-equity guard refuses to act on failed API reads.

Evidence

Every strategy graded by the same referee

A standalone verdict engine reruns each book through identical statistics: Sharpe confidence intervals from block bootstraps, a Deflated Sharpe Ratio penalized by every configuration ever tried (counted in a trial registry so K can't be fudged), and 10,000-path Monte Carlo outcome distributions. The labels are frozen at confirmed, promising, or insufficient, and no strategy grades its own homework. Killed ideas stay in the research journal next to the wins.

One year forward, 10,000 simulated paths
block-bootstrap Monte Carlo of realized daily returns · bands are the middle 50% and 90% of outcomes

Monte Carlo resamples what actually happened, in 21-day blocks — it preserves fat tails and vol clustering, but it cannot imagine a crash worse than its sample. Read the tails accordingly. Paper trading; not investment advice.

Risk Architecture

Built to lose less

The strategy is designed around capital preservation first. Three independent guardrails sit between the optimizer and the broker.

Vol-targeting overlay

Continuously scales the book toward a constant 20% annual volatility, de-risking into turbulence and parking the excess in cash.

Drawdown circuit breaker

Halts trading if the account breaches a hard drawdown limit, with a guard against false triggers from broker timeouts.

Concentration limit

Caps any single position's weight so one name cannot dominate the book, while exempting the cash sleeve.
